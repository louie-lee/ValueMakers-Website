Baby Shower & Pregnancy Announcement Ideas: Reveal Your Baby's Face

7 ideas your guests will actually remember · Updated July 2026

Balloon pops and confetti cannons reveal a color. But there's one reveal no guest has seen before: your baby's actual face. With an AI portrait generated from your 3D ultrasound, you can build your shower, announcement or reveal party around the most personal image possible — months before the birth.

First, create the portrait (2 minutes)

  1. Download BabyBloom free from the App Store.
  2. Upload your 3D ultrasound (step-by-step guide) — or both parents' photos if you don't have a scan.
  3. Download the 2K portrait. It's print-quality, so it works for everything below.

7 reveal ideas built around your baby's face

1. The face reveal easel

Frame the portrait, cover it with a curtain or wrapping, and make the unveiling the climax of your shower. Guests have seen cake cuttings; nobody has watched a curtain drop on the face of a baby who hasn't been born yet.

2. "Guess who they look like" game

Print Mom and Dad's childhood photos next to the AI portrait and have guests vote: whose nose, whose eyes, whose lips? BabyBloom's resemblance breakdown (Mom 54% / Dad 46%) is the answer card you reveal at the end.

3. Scan → portrait side-by-side announcement

The classic social announcement, upgraded: post the golden 3D scan next to the lifelike portrait with "Coming this November." The before/after is an instant scroll-stopper — and after the birth, add the real newborn photo as the third panel.

4. Grandparent reveal gift

Frame the portrait and gift-wrap it for the future grandparents. Film the unwrapping. This is the one that produces tears — have tissues ready.

5. Nursery wall first portrait

Print the 2K portrait as the first piece of nursery art — "the first photo in the album." After birth, hang the newborn photo beside it.

6. Gender reveal with a face

Instead of pink-or-blue confetti, reveal the portrait itself — the face is the reveal. Generate the portrait with your baby's gender selected and let guests meet them in one moment.

7. Baby shower thank-you cards

Print the portrait on thank-you cards: "Can't wait to meet you all — see you soon! — Baby." A keepsake guests genuinely keep.

💡 Timing tip: for the best portrait, use a scan from weeks 26–32 — the window when facial features are clearest. See the best time for a 3D ultrasound.

Why it works

Reveals succeed on emotion, and nothing is more emotional than a face. A color announcement says what; a portrait says who. Your guests aren't celebrating an abstract event anymore — they're meeting someone.
