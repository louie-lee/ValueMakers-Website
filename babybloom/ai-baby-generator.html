AI Baby Generator: See Your Future Baby From Mom & Dad's Photos

For couples who can't stop wondering · Updated July 2026

Every couple has played the game: your eyes, my smile, hopefully your hair. An AI baby generator turns that game into a picture. Upload one photo of each parent, and in seconds you're looking at a realistic face that's unmistakably a little bit of both of you.

How does an AI baby generator work?

Under the hood, the AI analyzes both parents' facial features — eye shape and color, nose structure, lip curve, face shape, skin tone — and blends them the way genetics might: not a 50/50 average, but a plausible combination where some features come through stronger than others.

BabyBloom's Parent Photo Mode goes a step further with a resemblance breakdown: alongside the generated baby face, you'll see exactly whose features dominated — say, Mom 54% / Dad 46%. It's the part everyone screenshots.

How to get the most realistic result

  • Use clear, front-facing photos of each parent — good lighting, face unobstructed.
  • One face per photo. Crop out friends, sunglasses and heavy filters.
  • Pick your baby's gender in the app to guide the generation.
  • Try a few photo pairs. Different source photos surface different feature blends — part of the fun.

Is an AI baby generator accurate?

It's entertainment with a scientific flavor — real genetics are far more complex than blending two photos, and even siblings from the same parents look different. Treat the result as one beautiful possibility, not a prophecy.
