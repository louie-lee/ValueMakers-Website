How to Put Motivational Quotes on Your iPhone Home Screen

Widget setup in under a minute · Updated July 2026

You check your phone dozens of times a day. Each unlock is either a slot machine of notifications — or a chance to see the one sentence you chose to live by today. A motivational quotes widget turns your most repeated habit into your most reliable source of fuel.

Why a widget beats an app you have to open

  • Zero friction. You don't have to remember to open anything — the quote is just there, on the home screen.
  • Spaced repetition. Seeing your quote of the day 30–50 times quietly drills it into your thinking.
  • Interrupts the doomscroll. The widget sits exactly where your thumb was headed anyway.

Set up the MOTIVE widget on iPhone (60 seconds)

  1. Install MOTIVE free from the App Store and pick your first quote of the day.
  2. Long-press any empty spot on your home screen until the icons jiggle.
  3. Tap the Edit → Add Widget button in the corner.
  4. Search for MOTIVE in the widget gallery.
  5. Swipe to choose a size — small for a punchline, medium for the full quote — then tap Add Widget.
  6. Tap Done. Your quote of the day now lives on your home screen and updates as you pick new quotes.
💡 Android user? Same idea: long-press the home screen → Widgets → MOTIVE. Get MOTIVE on Google Play.

Widget placement tips that actually matter

  • First page, top half. Put the widget where your eyes land on unlock — not on page three.
  • Pair it with your problem app. Place the widget right above Instagram or TikTok. Your quote gets the first word before the feed does.
  • Refresh it daily. The widget shows the quote you chose — picking a fresh one each morning (the Daily 5 ritual) keeps it from becoming wallpaper.

Make it a system, not a decoration

The widget is one layer of MOTIVE's motivation loop: morning Daily 5 → your pick → widget + reminders through the day → Night Review before bed. Words you see repeatedly become the way you talk to yourself. Choose them deliberately.
