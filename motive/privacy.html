MOTIVE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

The short version: MOTIVE does not require an account. What you write and choose in the app is stored on your device. We collect a small amount of anonymous usage data to keep the app working and to improve it, and the app may show ads from third-party partners, subject to your device's privacy settings. We don't sell your personal information.

MOTIVE ("the App") is developed and operated by Value Makers Inc. ("we", "us"), the data controller for the information described here. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information you provide

The App doesn't ask you to create an account. Anything you enter in the App — such as a goal, a daily action, your answers in the evening review, and any notes you write — is stored on your device. We don't collect it or see it, unless you choose a feature that expressly sends content to us for processing. If such a feature is available, it is optional and clearly marked, and content sent to us is used only to provide that feature.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most apps, MOTIVE uses third-party services to understand how the App is used and to keep it reliable. These services may collect information such as device type, operating system version, app version, general location derived from your network connection, a randomly generated app identifier, and in-app events (for example, that a screen was opened or a feature was used). This data is aggregated or pseudonymous and is not used to identify you personally. We process it on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating and improving the App.

If the App encounters a technical problem, an error report containing technical details about the device and the state of the App may be sent to us so we can fix it.

3. Advertising

The free version of the App may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners. These partners may use information such as your device's advertising identifier and coarse location, in accordance with your device settings. On iOS, the App asks for permission before accessing the advertising identifier, and you can change your choice at any time in your device settings. Ads may be less relevant if permission is not granted, but the App works the same way. Where consent is required for personalized advertising, we rely on the choice you make in that prompt or in your device settings. Subscribers to MOTIVE PRO do not see ads.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by the app store you downloaded the App from (Apple App Store or Google Play) and a payment-management service we use to validate purchases. We don't receive or store your payment details. We may receive a confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock the corresponding features.

5. Notifications and alarms

The App can send you reminders and alarms you set. These are scheduled on your device and are not used to collect information. You can turn them off at any time in the App or in your device settings.

6. Third-party services

The services described above are provided by companies that are contractually required to handle data only for the purpose of providing their service to us. They have their own privacy policies, which govern their handling of data.

We currently use Google AdMob (advertising), Amplitude (usage analytics), Sentry (error reporting), and RevenueCat (purchase validation). Google's use of data from apps that use its advertising services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The services we use may change from time to time.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. You can erase everything the App has stored by using the reset option in the App's settings or by uninstalling the App. Usage and error data held by our service providers is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, generally no longer than two years, after which it is deleted or aggregated. Purchase records are kept for as long as required for accounting and to honor your purchase.

8. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries that may not have the same data-protection laws as your country. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the providers' certified frameworks for such transfers.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, if higher), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict its use, to object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because the App stores your content on your device and the data we hold is pseudonymous, most requests can be fulfilled by using the App's reset option or your device settings. For anything else, contact us at the address below and we will respond within the time required by law.

We do not sell personal information and have not done so in the preceding twelve months. Sharing an advertising identifier with advertising partners may be considered "sharing" under certain laws; you can opt out through the advertising permission on your device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
