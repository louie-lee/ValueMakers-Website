Focus Mode

Your Devotion Blocks the Noise

When you begin a devotion, the distracting apps you choose are temporarily blocked with Apple's built-in Screen Time control. You can even keep apps locked each day until devotion is done. No willpower required — just space to be present with God.

One Verse a Day

Scripture, Slowly

No endless feeds. No notifications competing for your attention. Each day brings one carefully chosen passage and a gentle Read → Reflect → Respond → Pray flow. Write what stays with you — over time it becomes a quiet record of your walk through Scripture.

How It Works

One Verse. One Day. One Moment of Focus.

A complete daily devotion in about 3 minutes — with the noise switched off.

READ

One Passage

Open today's Scripture — one carefully chosen passage. Distracting apps go quiet.

REFLECT

Sit With the Words

Read slowly. Let what stands out stay with you — a word, a phrase, a quiet thought.

RESPOND

Write Briefly

Note what stayed with you. No pressure to be profound — honesty over eloquence.

PRAY

Close in Prayer

End in stillness. Your apps unlock; your reflection is saved to your journey.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about daily devotions, app blocking and quiet time.

How do I start a daily devotional as a beginner?
Start small: one passage, a short reflection, a brief prayer — about 3 minutes. Bible Focus structures it for you with a daily Read → Reflect → Respond → Pray flow. Learn more →
Can an app really block distractions during my quiet time?
Yes — Bible Focus uses Apple's built-in Screen Time control. When you begin a devotion, the apps you chose are temporarily blocked until you finish. Learn more →
What does "daily devotion unlocks apps" mean?
You can keep chosen apps (like social media) locked each day until your devotion is complete — Scripture first, feeds second. Learn more →
How long does a daily devotion take?
About 3 minutes — meaningful but small enough for busy mornings. You can return later to reread or update your reflection. Learn more →
Does Bible Focus have streaks or social features?
No — by design. No streak pressure, no feeds. A calm calendar records completed days, and your reflections stay private. Learn more →
Which Bible verses will I read?
One carefully chosen passage each day — like Psalm 46:10, "Be still, and know that I am God." One verse read slowly beats ten chapters rushed. Learn more →
Is Bible Focus free?
Yes — free on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 16 or later), with optional premium features. Get it on the App Store →
Will it help me spend less time on social media?
That's the heart of it: less social media, more God. Blocking the feeds during (or until) devotion replaces the morning scroll with the morning Word. Learn more →
