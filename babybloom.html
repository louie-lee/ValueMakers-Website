FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about turning ultrasounds into baby pictures and predicting your baby's face.

How do I turn my ultrasound into a baby picture?
Upload a photo of your 3D or 4D ultrasound to BabyBloom, choose your preferences, and the AI reads the facial geometry in your scan — nose shape, lip curve, eye depth — to generate a realistic 2K picture of your baby's face in about a minute. Learn more →
Can AI really predict what my baby will look like?
It's a realistic preview, not a crystal ball. From a 3D ultrasound the AI works from your actual baby's facial structure, so core features translate well; from parent photos it blends both faces into a plausible prediction. Learn more →
How does the baby face generator from parents' photos work?
Upload one clear photo of each parent, and BabyBloom's AI blends your features into a predicted baby face — with a resemblance breakdown showing exactly whose features shine through (say, Mom 54% / Dad 46%). Learn more →
Will my baby actually look like the 3D ultrasound?
Babies usually match their 3D scan in core structure — nose, lips, cheeks, jawline — but scans can't show skin tone, hair or expression. That's exactly the gap BabyBloom's AI fills. Learn more →
What's the best week of pregnancy for a 3D ultrasound?
Weeks 26–32 are the sweet spot: enough facial fat for defined features, enough room for a face-forward pose. Scans from this window make the best AI portraits. Learn more →
What can I do with my baby's AI portrait?
Baby shower reveals, pregnancy announcements, nursery prints, keepsake albums — the 2K portrait is yours to download and share however you like. Learn more →
Is BabyBloom free to download?
Yes — BabyBloom is free to download on iPhone, iPad and Android, with premium options inside the app for generating portraits. App Store → · Google Play →
Which ultrasound images work best — 2D, 3D or 4D?
3D and 4D scans work best because they capture facial depth. A clear, face-forward scan from weeks 26–32 gives the most accurate portrait — and if you don't have one, Parent Photo Mode works with just two photos. Learn more →
