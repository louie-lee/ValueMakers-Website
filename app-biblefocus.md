## App Store Description (key points, verbatim excerpts)

"Bible Focus is designed for moments when you want to slow down, open Scripture, and give God your full attention — without distractions."

- **Daily Scripture, one moment at a time** — one carefully chosen Bible passage each day; no endless feeds, no competing notifications
- **Reflect in your own words** — brief written reflection after reading; "no pressure to write something profound"; reflections become "a gentle record of your journey through Scripture"
- **Block distractions during quiet time** — built-in Screen Time control; when you begin a devotion, distracting apps are temporarily blocked; "This isn't about productivity. It's about creating space — free from noise — to be present with God."
- **~3 minutes a day** — fits busy mornings, quiet evenings, small pauses
- **Calm, minimal design** — no social features, no streak pressure
- **Who it's for** — anyone building a consistent Scripture habit, new to devotions or returning after a break
- Tagline: "One verse. One day. One moment of focus."

# Keyword Research & SEO Strategy (2026-07)

## Market Context

Devotional app space is crowded (YouVersion, Lectio 365, Quiet Time by Word of Life, Daily Bible) and "daily devotional" head terms are dominated by ministries (Navigators, Cru rank for how-to content). BUT no major player owns the **"blocks distractions / phone-free quiet time"** angle — Bible Focus's literal differentiator (bundle id: bible.focus.block.apps). Strategy: **lead with the distraction-blocking wedge + capture proven how-to question keywords with guide content.** The app's anti-streak, anti-feed positioning is also a strong counter-programming message against YouVersion fatigue.
