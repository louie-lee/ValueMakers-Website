## App Store Description (verbatim)

See your baby's face before they're born.

Meet your little one before the big day. BabyBloom uses advanced AI to transform your 3D ultrasound into a stunning portrait of your future baby — so you can see who's coming before they arrive.

**Ultrasound to Baby Portrait**
Upload your 3D ultrasound and watch it transform into a beautiful, portrait-quality 2K image of your baby's face. Our AI reads facial geometry — nose shape, lip curve, eye depth — to generate a prediction that feels truly personal.

**Predict from Parent Photos**
No ultrasound yet? Upload both parents' photos and discover what your future baby might look like. See whose features shine through with our resemblance breakdown — a little bit of both.

**How it works**
1. Upload your 3D ultrasound or both parents' photos
2. Select gender and preferences
3. AI generates your baby's predicted face
4. Download your portrait and share the moment

**Why BabyBloom?** Every pregnancy is a journey full of wonder and anticipation. BabyBloom gives you a first glimpse of the little one you've been waiting for — turning your ultrasound into a keepsake portrait you'll treasure forever.

The perfect addition to any baby shower, nursery, or pregnancy announcement. Share your baby's predicted face with family and friends and let the excitement begin.
