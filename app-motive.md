# Keyword Research & SEO Strategy (2026-07)

## Market Context

The "motivational quotes" web space is dominated by listicle content (BrainyQuote, Goodreads, uncommonquotes.com "300 quotes" posts) and the app space by "Motivation – Daily Quotes" (motivation.app, which also runs a content blog for SEO). Pure "motivational quotes" head terms are extremely high-volume but low-conversion (list-seekers, not app-installers). Strategy: **target "ongoing need" intent — people who want motivation delivered daily (widget, reminder, morning routine) or motivation for a specific struggle (gym, hard times) — where an app is the natural answer, not a list.**
